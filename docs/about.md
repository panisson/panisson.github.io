# About

I am a Principal Research Scientist and Team Lead at Intesa Sanpaolo AI Research in Turin. My work bridges Machine Learning, Network Science, and Data Science, with a strong emphasis on human-centric, trustworthy AI. I design and deploy methodologies that advance explainability, fairness, and transparency, translating research into accountable systems aligned with societal values. I lead industrial collaborations bringing generative AI to high-stakes domains such as legal document analysis and anti-financial-crime compliance, pairing technical innovation with regulatory alignment. I also serve as PI of the EU-funded PRE-ACT consortium—running a Phase III trial on AI-assisted breast radiotherapy decisions—in a WP on Fair, Transparent & Ethical AI, building bias-aware evaluation pipelines and practical guidelines.

Over the past 15+ years, I’ve contributed to both foundational research and scalable systems in complex socio-technical settings, including real-time data pipelines and graph-based learning for behavior modeling and risk detection. My research spans graph neural networks, XAI, and LLM-powered document analysis (RAG), and has appeared in venues such as NeurIPS, KDD, TKDE, TMLR, and WWW.

Before Intesa Sanpaolo, I spent 4 years as a Principal Research Scientist and team lead at CENTAI, and a decade as a Senior Research Scientist at ISI Foundation, engineering tools and ML models for human behavior, epidemic dynamics, and digital trace analytics; I’ve also taught Data Mining courses regularly since 2013 at the University of Turin. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Turin, and I work fluently across academic and industrial ecosystems in finance, healthcare, and public interest tech, mentoring teams and building explainable ML solutions that move from lab to production responsibly.
